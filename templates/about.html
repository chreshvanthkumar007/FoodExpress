About FoodExpress

Delivering Happiness, One Meal at a Time

FoodExpress is an innovative college mini project designed to connect foodies with the best kitchens and restaurants in town with speed, simplicity, and delight.

30-Min Delivery

Our intelligent routing and prioritized kitchen dispatch ensure your meals reach your table piping hot.

Hygiene Certified

We partner exclusively with verified culinary kitchens that comply with top-tier food safety standards.

Live Order Tracking

Stay informed from the moment your food is accepted in the kitchen right up to your front door.

How FoodExpress Works

01
Choose Food

Explore delicious cuisines, authentic Biryanis, Pizzas, Burgers, and curries from top partner restaurants.

02
Place Order

Review your cart items, select Cash on Delivery or fast Demo Online Payment, and confirm your order in one click.

03
Get Food Delivered

Sit back, relax, and track your delivery in real-time as our delivery partners bring fresh hot meals to your door.

About This Mini Project

This application is engineered as a full-stack mini project using Python 3, Flask framework, and MySQL 8.0 relational database with Flask-SQLAlchemy ORM.
